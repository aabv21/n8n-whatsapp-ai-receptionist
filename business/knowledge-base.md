# Cumbident knowledge base

Structured facts — the address, prices, hours, and payment methods — live in `business.json`, not here.

## About

Cumbident is a dental studio in Barranquilla, Colombia.

## Parking

Parking is free for patients.

## Children

Cumbident sees children starting at age 4.

## Insurance and invoices

Cumbident doesn't accept insurance, but issues an invoice that patients can use for reimbursement from their own insurance provider.

## Cancellations

Patients can cancel by WhatsApp. Please give at least 24 hours notice.

## What the assistant can't do

The assistant can't diagnose a condition or give medical advice.
